## 2. REEL PARALLAX ANIMATION MECHANISMS

### 📜 1. Five Scroll-Driven Parallax Stages (`ScrollParallaxCoffeeShowcase.tsx`)
- **Stage 1: Steam Moves (אדים נעים):** Ambient rising steam particles over the ceramic mug.
- **Stage 2: Milk Flows In (חלב מוקצף נשפך פנימה):** Pouring stream simulation creating rosette latte art.
- **Stage 3: Ice Cubes Drop (קוביות קרח נופלות):** Falling transparent 3D ice cubes with collision physics.
- **Stage 4: Sugar Dissolves (סוכר מתמוסס):** Dissolving sugar crystal particles.
- **Stage 5: Coffee Beans Float (פולי קפה צפים ב-Parallax):** Multi-layer floating roasted coffee beans rotating and moving on scroll.

### 💻 2. Rustic Dark Wood & Warm Bokeh Aesthetics
- **Color Palette:** Warm espresso brown, mocha, cream, beige, obsidian slate, and golden ambient glowing orbs.
- **Background Ambiance:** Soft blurred warm pendant light bokeh effect.
- **Glassmorphic Cards:** Translucent `backdrop-blur-2xl` cards with refractive borders.
