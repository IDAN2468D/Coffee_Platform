# Gourmet Coffee Customer Loyalty & VIP Order Scoring Algorithm

## 1. Loyalty Score Calculation
- **Order Value (40 Points Max):**
  - Above ₪250: 40 pts
  - ₪100 – ₪250: 25 pts
  - Under ₪100: 15 pts

- **Subscription Status (30 Points Max):**
  - Active Monthly Beans Subscription: 30 pts
  - One-time order: 10 pts

- **Engagement & Special Requests (30 Points Max):**
  - Saved Custom Roast/Brew Profile: 15 pts
  - Bio-Energy Matcher Usage: 15 pts

## 2. Customer Tiering & VIP Privileges
- **Platinum VIP (Score >= 80):** Free Express Courier delivery + complimentary single-origin sample bag + priority barista preparation queue.
- **Gold Coffee Connoisseur (Score 50 - 79):** Complimentary milk upgrade + 10% discount on V60 equipment.
- **Silver Coffee Enthusiast (Score < 50):** Standard rewards program points.
