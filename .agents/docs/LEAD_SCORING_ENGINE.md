# Lead Scoring Engine Specification

## Scoring Algorithm
- **Budget (40 points):**
  - ₪10M+: 40 pts
  - ₪5M - ₪10M: 25 pts
  - Under ₪5M: 15 pts
- **Timeline (30 points):**
  - Under 3 months: 30 pts
  - 3 - 6 months: 20 pts
  - Over 6 months: 10 pts
- **Contact Details (30 points):**
  - Phone + Email provided: 30 pts
  - Phone only: 20 pts

## Action Matrix
- **Hot (Score >= 75):** Trigger instant SMS to Realtor & WhatsApp auto-welcome message.
- **Warm (Score 50-74):** Follow-up within 24 hours with tailored property catalog.
- **Cold (Score < 50):** Monthly newsletter list.
