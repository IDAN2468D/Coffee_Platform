### 🌰 2. Multi-Layer Parallax Roasted Bean Canvas (`ParallaxBeanCanvas.tsx`)
- **3D Floating Coffee Beans:** HTML5 Canvas particle rendering of floating roasted coffee beans rotating and moving with window scroll.
- **Parallax Scroll Factor:** Background beans move at 0.2x scroll speed, foreground beans at 0.5x scroll speed.

### 💨 3. Ambient Steam Particle Simulation (`SteamParticlesCanvas.tsx`)
- Continuous rising steam specks over glassmorphic coffee cards.
