# Gemini 3.5 Multimodal Barista Ultra Skill

## Capabilities & Modules
1. **Voice Assistant (הזמנה קולית):** Real-time speech-to-intent entity extraction for Hebrew coffee orders.
2. **Vision Bean Analysis (זיהוי פולים):** Image analysis of coffee bean photos to identify Agtron roast index, particle microns, SCA cupping score, and flavor profiles.
3. **Acoustic Grind & Flow Analyzer (ניתוח אקוסטי):** Grinder burr noise Hz frequency parsing, particle micron estimation (380µm), burr dullness alerts, and extraction drip frequency channeling detection.
4. **Bloom Degassing Freshness Vision (בעבוע Bloom):** Visual CO2 bubble degassing volume calculation (cm³), post-roast days estimation, and pour-over bloom duration tips.
5. **AR Latte Art Pour Guidance (לאטה ארט AR):** Real-time pour path overlay, pitcher angle (degrees), milk velocity (cm/s), and symmetry scoring.
6. **Coffee Mixology & Signature Drink Creator (מיקסולוגיית קפה):** AI gourmet coffee cocktail and zero-proof mocktail recipe builder with 1-click cart integration.
