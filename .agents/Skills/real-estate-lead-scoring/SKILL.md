# Real Estate Lead Scoring Skill

## Rules
- Budget >= ₪10M: +40 pts
- Timeline <= 3 months: +30 pts
- Valid Phone + Email: +30 pts

## Classifications
- **Hot (>=75):** Immediate rep call + WhatsApp catalog.
- **Warm (50-74):** Follow up within 24h.
- **Cold (<50):** Email nurture.
