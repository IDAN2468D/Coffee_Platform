# Coffee & Food Sommelier Skill

## Rules & Pairing Taxonomy
- **High Acidity Light Roasts (Ethiopia, Kenya):** Pair best with sweet, fruity, or cream-based pastries (e.g., Lemon Tart, Berry Cheesecake).
- **Nutty & Chocolatey Medium Roasts (Colombia, Guatemala):** Pair best with buttery croissant, toasted nuts, and praline desserts.
- **Full-Bodied Dark Roasts (Sumatra, Dark Espresso Blends):** Pair best with dark chocolate (70%+), tiramisu, and savory brunch items.
- Always output pair ratings (1-100%) and brief gourmet explanation in Hebrew RTL.
