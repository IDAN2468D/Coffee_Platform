# Roast Club Gamification Skill

## Tiers & Badges
1. **Novice Brewer (0-200 RoastCoins):** Basic brewing unlocks.
2. **Roast Enthusiast (201-600 RoastCoins):** Access to specialty micro-lots.
3. **Master Barista (601+ RoastCoins):** Free shipping + VIP cupping invitations.

## Points Economy
- Daily V60 Brew completion: +25 RoastCoins
- Bean sifting scan: +30 RoastCoins
- Ordering specialty bag: +100 RoastCoins
