# Coffee Flavor Matcher Skill

## Core Rules & Flavor Profiles
1. **Midnight Espresso Blend:**
   - Roast Level: 10/12 (Dark Italian Roast)
   - Flavor Notes: Dark Cocoa, Roasted Hazelnut, Caramel
   - Best Paired With: Whole Milk, Double Espresso Shot
2. **Honey Oak Cortado:**
   - Roast Level: 7/12 (Medium-Dark Oak Roast)
   - Flavor Notes: Wild Honey, Toasted Almond, Oak Wood
   - Best Paired With: Oatly Oat Milk, 2-3 Espresso Shots
3. **Lavender Fields Latte:**
   - Roast Level: 6/12 (Medium French Roast)
   - Flavor Notes: French Lavender, Madagascar Vanilla, Cream
   - Best Paired With: Oat Milk or Almond Milk (Vegan Friendly)
4. **V60 Single Origin Pour-over:**
   - Roast Level: 4/12 (Light City Roast)
   - Flavor Notes: Jasmine, Citrus Bergamot, Wild Berry
   - Best Paired With: Pure Hot Water (92°C), No Milk
