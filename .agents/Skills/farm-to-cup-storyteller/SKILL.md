# Farm-to-Cup Storyteller Skill

## Storytelling Elements
- **Altitude Telemetry:** (e.g. 1,900 - 2,200m above sea level). Higher altitude = denser bean, brighter acidity.
- **Process Method:** Natural (Sun-dried), Washed, Anaerobic Fermentation.
- **Producer Bio:** Highlighting sustainable farming, fair trade pricing, and cupping scores (SCA 86+).
