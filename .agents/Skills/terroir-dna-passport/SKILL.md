# 🌱 Micro-Lot Terroir & Genetic DNA Passport Skill

## 1. Overview
Generates an interactive, transparent provenance passport for single-origin micro-lots, detailing altitude, volcanic soil geology, farmer profit margins, and botanical lineage.

## 2. Micro-Lot Parameters & Direct Trade Formula
- **Direct Trade Premium:**
  $$\text{Farmer Payout Margin} = \frac{\text{Direct Price} - \text{C-Market Baseline}}{\text{C-Market Baseline}} \times 100\% \ge +300\%$$
- **Terroir Physics:**
  - Elevation: $1{,}600 - 2{,}300\,\text{MASL}$ (Dense bean structure, high sucrose formation)
  - Volcanic Soil Composition: Basalt, Andesite, Organic Humus, $\text{pH } 5.5 - 6.5$
  - Varietal Genetics: Geisha (Panama T2722), Ethiopian Heirloom, Pink Bourbon, SL28, Sudan Rume
